PRINT TOKEN

Grayprints (GPs) are single-use crafting documents in My Last Match that originate from a parent Whiteprint. Each Grayprint represents one authorized attempt to craft a specific item. The PRINT token gives you exclusive access to a hand-picked selection of some of the most important in-game technologies out there — core systems that seriously boost your survival and power progression, like the Geiger Counter, Faraday Casing, and Rubber Accumulator.

Your dedicated storage container at the Hospital gives you free and secure storage for all your Grayprints. Once your order is submitted, your freshly printed Grayprints will be placed directly into your personal container, ready to unlock incredible new crafting possibilities for your character. Please remember that Grayprints provide the exclusive know-how and blueprint — they do not include the crafting materials which you will need to gather in the game to unleash the potential of these powerful technologies.


PRE-STEP: OPEN YOUR PRINTER ACCOUNT
1. Go to the Hospital in My Last Match
2. Speak with the NPC at the counter
3. Sign Smart Contract for a small fee in QUs
4. Your PRINTER account is now active and your personal storage container is unlocked
This is a one-time setup. Your storage is permanently assigned to your Wallet ID.
HOW TO PURCHASE GRAYPRINTS
1. Purchase PRINT tokens on QUBICTRADE.COM
2. *Send PRINT tokens to the shop address
3. Select GPs and submit the order
4. We notify you via Discord once your GPs are ready in your personal container
You must send the tokens using the same Wallet ID your personal container is assigned to!
